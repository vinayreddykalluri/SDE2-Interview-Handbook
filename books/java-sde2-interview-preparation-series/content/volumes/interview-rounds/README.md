# Interview Rounds Series

This shelf covers the interview rounds that are not about code or architecture but still decide the level of an offer: behavioral, leadership, and hiring-manager rounds.

## Reading order

| Order | Workspace | Focus |
|---:|---|---|
| IR-01 | `IR-01-behavioral-and-leadership-interviews/` | the signals a behavioral round scores, story banks, STAR delivery, conflict, failure, mentoring, company values, and the hiring-manager round |

## How to study each book

1. Read a chapter, then apply it to your own stories the same day; the book is only useful with your material.
2. Run the Java companion to see coverage, timing, and pronoun checks on the sample bank, then adapt it to your own.
3. Attempt the drills in writing before opening the worked solutions.
4. Rehearse aloud and record yourself; delivery problems are invisible on paper.

## Scope boundary

IR-01 does not teach technical content. Coding, design, and framework rounds are covered by the other segments; this segment prepares the evidence those rounds cannot show.
