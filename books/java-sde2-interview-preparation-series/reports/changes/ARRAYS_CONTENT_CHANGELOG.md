## Summary

The enhancement concentrates on foundational strength and interview reasoning rather than adding unrelated algorithms. ArrayList and safe sorting remain because they are necessary Java interview tools; deep collection internals, binary-search catalogs, monotonic structures, online range trees, and dynamic programming remain in their dedicated volumes.
