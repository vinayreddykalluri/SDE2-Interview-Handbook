## Boundary ownership

- Full XOR families, subset masks, popcount tricks, and bitwise optimization remain in the Bit Manipulation mini-book.
- Generalized answer-space search remains in the Binary Search mini-book.
- Array prefix-modulo problem families remain in Arrays; modular hashing remains in Hashing.
- Full recursive exponentiation belongs in Recursion; this book keeps the numeric invariant self-contained.
- Advanced theorem-heavy number theory, cryptography, CRT, and competitive-programming-only methods remain out of scope.
