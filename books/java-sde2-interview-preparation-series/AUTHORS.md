## Credit rules

- Every accepted original prose, example, exercise, solution, or diagram contribution is eligible for individual credit.
- Small corrections remain permanently attributed through Git history and may be summarized here when they materially improve a release.
- Co-authorship of a chapter or volume requires substantial original educational content, not only proofreading or formatting.
- AI-assisted contributions must be reviewed, understood, and owned by the human submitter; an AI system is not listed as an author.
- The contributor must identify third-party source material and confirm that its license is compatible.
- Attribution corrections are treated as high-priority editorial issues.

## How to be added

In a pull request, complete the authorship section in the template and add or update one row in this file when the work qualifies for publication-level credit. State the name and link exactly as they should appear. The Editor-in-Chief confirms scope; the Chief Auditor confirms originality, accuracy, and validation evidence.
