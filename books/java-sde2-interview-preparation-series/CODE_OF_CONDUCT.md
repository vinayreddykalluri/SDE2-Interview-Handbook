# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for readers and contributors regardless of experience, education, identity, background, location, or communication style.

This project exists to help people learn. Questions asked in good faith are welcome, including questions about material that others consider basic.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and explanations—not people.
- Assume good intent while still correcting harmful or inaccurate behavior.
- Welcome corrections and acknowledge sources and contributors.
- Use inclusive examples and language.
- Respect privacy, employer confidentiality, and interview integrity.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and relevant commercial affiliations.
- Accept editorial decisions gracefully and continue disagreement through evidence-based discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, humiliation, or persistent bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Plagiarism, false authorship, manipulated attribution, or removal of contributor credit.
- Sharing leaked interview banks, confidential employer material, proprietary code, or content without redistribution rights.
- Spam, undisclosed promotion, or disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), with the subject `Code of Conduct`. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

The Editor-in-Chief will review reports fairly and may remove content, close discussions, limit participation, or ban an account. When the Editor-in-Chief is involved in a report, an uninvolved trusted maintainer should review it where available.

Project maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and will not retaliate against good-faith reports.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-related public or private communication when a person represents the project.

## Attribution

This project-specific code is informed by widely used open-source community standards. It may be updated transparently through the governance process.
