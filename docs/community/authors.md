## Credit rules

- Original prose, examples, exercises, solutions, diagrams, and tooling remain attributable to their individual contributors.
- Small corrections are permanently credited through Git history.
- Chapter or volume co-authorship requires substantial original educational work.
- AI-assisted contributions must be reviewed, understood, disclosed, and owned by the human contributor; an AI system is not listed as an author.
- Third-party source material must be identified and license-compatible.
- Attribution corrections are high-priority editorial issues.

Contributors retain copyright in their original work and license accepted contributions under the repository's applicable CC BY 4.0 or MIT license.
